About the Hunt

The CTF Treasure Hunt is a one-night event that challenges your technical skills, problem-solving abilities, and teamwork. Navigate through a series of puzzles, decode hidden messages, and uncover secrets to claim victory and awesome prizes!

Perfect for developers, architects, DevOps engineers, product owners, and all tech enthusiasts at ING.

Rules & Guidelines

1

Team Composition

Teams of 3-5 members. You can register as a complete team or as an individual to be matched with others.

2

Duration

The hunt will last for the specified time period, with real-time updates on your progress.

3

Tools

You may use any online resources, programming tools, or software to solve challenges. Collaboration within teams is encouraged.

4

Scoring

Points are awarded based on challenge difficulty and completion time. Earlier completions receive bonus points.

5

Fair Play

No sharing solutions between teams. Admins will monitor progress and may provide hints if needed.
